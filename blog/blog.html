It's the morning and I'm on a roll! I have a few specific features I'd like to implement here that I was thinking about overnight and started working on this morning.

My goal now is to be able to post directly through the web interface (greetings from the dull void of VSCode!) and have a few different elements I can play around with. I'm thinking: title, text, author, timestamp, maybe show the generated color code, for fun?

Things I will need:
- Prompt button;
- Login/credential checker;

- JS to format post for DB;

I'm feeling good. I have a thing to work on that's enjoyable and gives me practice at a skill. I'm highly aware of the fact that blogs are no longer popular for online writing, that many platforms exist to do this much more quickly, and that social media has all but replaced them.
